## Ranked fix list

Severity: **B** = blocker, **U** = ugly, **P** = polish.

### B1. The View Transitions capture the old page, and a cold route flashes blank (B, every route)

**Skills:** Bao "Smooth View Transitions between pages"; gsap-react / emil (motion that explains the change).

`src/components/ui/RouteTransition.tsx:115-133` calls `flushSync(() => navigate(href))` inside `startViewTransition`. React Router 7.18 runs `navigate` in a transition, so `flushSync` commits nothing. The lazy route also has not loaded yet.

Instrumented on the built site:
- On all 9 navigations, the "new" snapshot still held the old page's h1.
- Recorded on a cold navigation from /our-story (scrolled) to /pricing:
  - 0-400 ms: the old page cross-fades into its own top.
  - ~1000 ms: the route commits.
  - 1100-1300 ms: a blank cream `RouteFallback` with the footer pulled up.
  - ~1400 ms: Pricing appears.
- Warm routes commit about 60 ms after capture, so the new page pops in inside the live pseudo-element.

**Fix:**
1. In the click handler, `await` the route's preloader first. `lib/lazyRoute.ts` already keeps one per path; export `preloadRoute(path)`.
2. Then call `document.startViewTransition(async () => { navigate(href); await routeCommitted(href); jumpTo(0); })`. `routeCommitted` resolves from a `useLayoutEffect` keyed on `pathname` in RouteTransition.
3. Or call the `navigate` inside `startTransition` and await the commit the same way.
4. Never let the VT start before the chunk is ready. Keep the 240 ms fade only as the fallback.
5. Re-record frames to verify.

### B2. First-screen content hidden until the reader scrolls (B: /pricing, /our-story, /contact)

**Skills:** Bao "Nothing is ever left hidden"; gsap-scrolltrigger (triggers already in view).

Blocks whose top sits in the bottom 10% of the first screen never reach `start: "top 90%"`:
- **/pricing**, 1366 and 1920: the three ladder headings ("Free, for as long as you like." and the rest) are at opacity 0. The reader sees a hairline and an empty band.
- **/our-story**, 1920: both letters' first paragraphs are at 0, so the bottom 280 px of the first screen is bare cloth.
- **/contact**, 1366 and 2560: "Or write directly" is at 0.

**Fix:** in `src/lib/scrollMotion.ts:112` `arrival()`, also play the tween at once when the trigger's top is above the window's bottom at refresh (`self.trigger.getBoundingClientRect().top < innerHeight`). Apply the same guard to `home/motion.ts` (`START = "top 92%"`, which has no guard at all) and `our-story/motion.ts:42/64`.

### B3. Layout shift on /samples and /reserve (B: performance)

- **/samples:** CLS 0.49 on a throttled phone, 0.06-0.09 on desktop. The sources are `DIV.postlist`, `DIV.reserve`, `DIV.body` and `.notecard`, from late webfonts or the book sizing after load.
  - **Fix:** reserve the book's box with aspect-ratio before the script measures. Self-host the Google fonts with `size-adjust` fallbacks. Load the notecard and reserve fonts before first layout.
- **/reserve:** CLS 0.21 at 1920 and 0.18 under reduced motion. The opening section (`sc-jePMYA`) reflows about 370 ms after load, likely from the account or founding-count state (`Reserve.tsx:526`).
  - **Fix:** render the final layout from the first paint (a reserved line height for `placesLine` and the account state) or swap the text in place.
- **/pricing:** CLS 0.16 on a throttled phone (`P.opening-lead`). **Fix:** same pattern as /reserve.

### B4. The cloth texture is the LCP and it is heavy (B: performance)

`public/brand/cloth-chocolate.webp` (1024², 295 KB) and `cloth-teal.webp` (1024², 364 KB) are CSS backgrounds that load late. Measured LCP under throttling:

| Page | Size | LCP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | 1366 | 9.4 s |
| /pricing | 390 | 4.4 s |
| /our-story | 390 | 5.1 s |

Home moves 1.9-2.3 MB at first load.

**Fixes:**
- Re-encode both tiles at 512² or 768² with q55-60 (target ≤ 70 KB). Add `<link rel="preload" as="image">` for the chocolate tile in `index.html`. Give every cloth section a solid `background-color` so the page paints before the tile.
- Let the Home h1 paint at full opacity before the rise. A mask rise with no opacity change keeps it the LCP.

### U3. /samples labels and targets (U: public/samples)

- **Targets:**

- **Page turn:** `.9s cubic-bezier(.45,.05,.25,1)`. **Fix:** use `house-move`. A page turn is fine at about 600 ms, since readers turn often (emil's frequency rule).
