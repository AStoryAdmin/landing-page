## How this was checked

- **Probe:** a Playwright probe in Edge 155 on 12 routes at 1366×768, 1920×1000, 2560×1300 and 390×844, plus a reduced-motion pass. It measured:
  - heading line counts, horizontal scroll and tap targets
  - content still faded on the first screen without scrolling, and after a full scroll
  - CLS, image weights and font weights
- **Throttled run:** LCP and CLS under Slow 4G and 4x CPU, on 7 routes at 390 and 1366.
- **View Transitions:** the `startViewTransition` callback was instrumented over 4 navigations. Frames were recorded on a cold /our-story → /pricing navigation.
- **/samples:** the controls, the "Read in large type" reader (focus moves in, Esc returns focus), and a scan of the built HTML for dev-only paths.
- **taste-skill `inspect.mjs`** ran on /, /how-it-works, /pricing, /our-story, /for-families and /samples.
- **Not verified:**
  - Edge zoomed out, and 3840 wide.
  - A real device or a real network.
  - Form submissions (none were sent).
  - The keyboard Tab pass was not repeated this round; the focus-ring code has not changed since round 1.

## Remaining fixes, ranked

### N1. Broken partner logos on /samples in production (blocker, public/samples)

`public/samples/index.html` (copied into `dist/samples/index.html`) has a footer pasted from the dev render. Its five `<img src="/src/assets/partners/*.svg">` paths exist only on the dev server. In the build they return `text/html` from the SPA fallback, so all five have `naturalWidth` 0.

The page shows five broken-image icons with alt text inside the white logo strip (screenshot: `r2/samples-footer.jpg` in the scratchpad). It is the only HTML file in `dist/` with a `/src/` path.

**Fix:**
1. Copy the five SVGs to `public/partners/`.
2. Point the five `src` attributes at `/partners/<name>.svg`.
3. Add a check to `qa/definitive/production-contracts.mjs` that fails when any `dist/**/*.html` contains `"/src/` or `/@`.

### N2. /samples shifts on a phone (ugly, public/samples)

Throttled at 390 (Slow 4G, 4x CPU), CLS is 0.27, which counts as poor. Almost all of it is one shift: `SECTION.bench` grows from 172 px to 422 px at about 3.3 s and pushes the book down. Unthrottled, the page measures 0.

**Fix:** give `.bench` (the title, book picker and swatches) its final height in CSS before fonts and script land. For example, `min-height` at the phone breakpoint, or set the picker and swatch rows to their final sizes in the static HTML.

### N4. /our-story and /reserve are slow to their largest paint (ugly, other pages)

Throttled LCP: /our-story 4.7 s at both 390 and 1366, /reserve 3.6 s, /samples 3.7-4.7 s. Images are light (/our-story's largest is 55 KB). The same timing holds under reduced motion, so the arrivals are not the cause.

The likely cause is scripts. /our-story preloads `supabase` (53 KB gzipped) through `contact` and `demoRequest`, on top of the 151 KB gzipped main bundle. The page re-renders when the route chunk lands.

**Fix:** import the contact and demo-request clients dynamically, on submit only. Check that the prerendered HTML matches the first client render, so hydration does not swap the largest block.

### N5. Small items (polish)

- **390 button pairs** on /pricing and /how-it-works stack as a full-width primary over a narrow secondary. Match Home: both full width.
- **/how-it-works at 390:** the teal sample-call section leaves about 200 px of empty teal before "Kept from every call". The sticky Start bar briefly covers "family has a way in." (inspector covered-text).
- **The /samples reader:**
  - It flattens the page into same-size paragraphs ("CHAPTER ONE", "01" and "WHERE HER STORY STARTS" all at body size).
  - On desktop it sits inside a drawn phone frame (hallmark gate 47).
  - **Fix:** keep heading levels and the date/answer pairing, and use a plain sheet on desktop.
- **`app/screens.tsx:280`:** `transition: background-size 900ms 700ms ease`. Move it to the house curve, or make it a one-time arrival.
- **Dead files:** delete `components/ui/Reveal.tsx` and the 14 unused `home/*.tsx` files so lint and future audits read only live code.
- **/care-communities:** CLS 0.088 at 1920 (`DIV.opening-sheet` at about 465 ms). Reserve the sheet's height.
- **Outside this site:** `/start` redirects to `app.astoryapp.com/create-account`, which loads the app's own `book/cloth-chocolate.webp` at 295 KB. That belongs to the web-app owner; the same re-encode applies there.
