# A Story sample books — reader panel, round 2

Same five readers and questions as round 1 (`panel-round1.md`), scored cold on the edited data files (carolyn.js, raymond.js, tom.js) read in page order, plus about 10 rendered spreads per book (Carolyn from `shots-r4`, Raymond and Tom from `shots-r3`): cover, front matter, contents, two memory spreads with plates, little questions, learned, people, letter, last page and back cover. Questions (a)–(d) are scored 1–10. (e) is best page / page to cut. Each mean is over 20 scores (5 readers × 4 questions). Limit: the screenshots stop at 800 px, so the bottom of each page wasn't visible. Page breaks were judged from the page numbers and the next spread.
