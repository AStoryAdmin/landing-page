- (a) 9 — I know him off the clock now. He'd "stand in the lake up to my knees and call it swimming". He makes "Coffee for Elena, then mine". He cried "at a gas station in Texarkana". Still, nine of the thirteen memories are the shop.

- (d) 8 — The headnotes narrate the recording: "he lowers his voice for the part about the deposit slip", "he closes it before the last part". That's the company watching him. I'd keep the listen cards. The voice is what goes first.
