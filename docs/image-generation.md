# Generated family scenes

Ten fictional scenes were generated with OpenAI ImageGen on 21 September 2026 from
the briefs in `image-prompts.md`. They are illustrative images, not real family or
customer photographs. The full-resolution PNG masters are in
`assets/mission-originals/`; the responsive WebP files are in `public/mission/`.
Each ID has 640, 1200 and 1800 named derivatives. Dimensions, hashes and placement
are recorded in `src/lib/assetRegistry.json` and `src/lib/mission.ts`.

- `H01` — Sarah and three-year-old Mia at the moonlit bedroom window (2019).
- `H02` — Walt and twelve-year-old Dan at the garage workbench (1984).
- `H03` — Dan and Sam, both about 26, laughing beside their overturned canoe (1998).
- `H04` — Walt telling Lily the lake monster story by the campfire (2024).
- `F01` — a grandmother unwrapping an A Story book; For families opening.
- `F02` — a grandfather and grandson with an A Story birthday book gift.
- `F03` — a daughter holding a phone with A Story while her mother tells a story.
- `F04` — a grandmother telling a story with A Story running on a phone on her lap.
- `F05` — three generations listening after holiday dinner; no phone or product.
- `F06` — a couple reminiscing over their wedding photograph on an A Story book.

Product placement is limited to present-day scenes. The four dated homepage
memories (1984, 1998, 2019 and 2024) remain product-free. The dinner phone was
removed at the founder's request because it felt intrusive. The gifting image's
extra hand was corrected; only two hands now support the book. Final editing
prompts and the rejected dinner treatment are in `product-image-prompts.json`.

The ten individual full-resolution PNGs are also exported with descriptive names
to the workspace's sibling `image for claude` folder. No overview collage is
included.

The homepage Hartley chronology is now consistent: Walt and Ruth are young adults
in 1952; their son Dan is 12 in 1984, graduates in 1994, and is 26 on the 1998
canoe trip. Walt is about 55 in 1984 and about 95 in the 2024 campfire scene.
Mia is three in 2019 and ten in 2026. The five occasion cards illustrate other
fictional families and need not share the Hartleys' ages.

The source brief proposed different ages for Walt and Dan in two scenes. Those
were adjusted to preserve one believable family timeline. The 2019 book spread
now tells the moon-window story shown in `H01`; the old toy-truck text no longer
accompanies that photograph. The optional laundry image remains the existing
`35`, as the brief already considered it suitable.
