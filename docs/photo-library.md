# Photo library

62 images, each at 640 / 1200 / 1800px, in `public/mission/` as `<id>-<width>.webp`.
Full catalogue with source hashes, focal requirements and provenance:
`src/lib/assetRegistry.json`.

Contact sheets in this folder — look at these before assigning anything.

All photography is approved synthetic illustration. It is not customer photography and
no image depicts a documented family. Captions in the registry describe an *intended*
era, not a real date.

---

## The library is two libraries

**`01`–`35` — archival.** Black-and-white film, faded 70s colour, historical prints,
kitchens and porches and station wagons. This is the set the site uses and the set that
carries the product argument. The imperfection is an advantage.

**`41`–`52`, `S01`–`S08` — contemporary.** Phone snapshots: dorm rooms, flat-pack
furniture, ski days, campfires. Almost all are marked "approved reserve" and unused.
They are weaker and read closer to stock. They exist to make one argument — today's
ordinary moments become tomorrow's missing stories — and if the design doesn't make
that argument, they should be retired rather than scattered for variety.

**`36`–`40` — the book.** Teal. See the open decision in the brief.

## Rules that come with the library

- Approved A Story assets only. No stock, no new AI families, no Unsplash.
- Natural proportions for archival prints. Controlled crops are allowed only for page
  introductions and small thumbnails; book objects always contain.
- Per-image focal requirements are recorded in the registry — e.g. `10` reads
  *"keep coats, umbrella, school bag and shoes; door edge optional."* Read them before
  cropping.
- No collage, ghost images, fake print aging, decorative rotation or photographic
  overlays. Historical prints may take a restrained warm edge; modern photographs are
  borderless.
- Alt text is in the registry and should be used, not rewritten from scratch.
