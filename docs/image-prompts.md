# Image briefs — the Hartley family (homepage examples)

The homepage follows one fictional family (`src/lib/homeExamples.ts`). The four
required homepage scenes and six For families scenes were generated and installed
on 21 September 2026. See `docs/image-generation.md` for their final IDs, masters,
and family timeline corrections. The briefs below remain creative source material;
their original ages and placeholder references are historical.

**For every image:** candid, not posed; natural or practical light; real, slightly
untidy homes; no one looking at the camera; no text, logos or watermarks; no phones
held toward the camera; skin, hands and faces photographically correct. Contemporary
images should look like a good phone photo (not glossy stock). Archival images should
look like the film stock of their year, without fake scratches or sepia filters.

---

### 1 · `mum-and-daughter` — "what she said the night she asked where the moon goes"
Used in: What gets lost (row 1), the book (2019 spread).
Currently: `41` (a toddler with a toy truck).

> Candid phone photograph, evening, a mother kneeling beside her three-year-old daughter
> at a bedroom window, both looking up at the moon, the girl mid-question with one hand
> on the glass, the mother listening with a small notebook half-open in her other hand.
> Warm lamp light inside, blue dusk outside, children's drawings taped to the wall,
> slightly messy room. Shot from behind and to the side so faces are three-quarter.
> Natural colour, shallow depth of field, 4:5 portrait.

### 2 · `grandpa-workbench` — "the story Grandpa only tells with a screwdriver in his hand"
Used in: What gets lost (row 2), the book (1984 spread).
Currently: `08` (a man repairing a fan, 1984).

> Candid photograph, a grandfather in his late seventies at a cluttered garage
> workbench repairing an old desk fan, mid-sentence, eyebrows raised, while his
> twelve-year-old grandson sits on an upturned crate holding the screwdriver, laughing.
> Pegboard of tools, jars of screws, a single work lamp, dust in the light. Warm
> practical light, natural colour, 3:2 landscape. The story is in his face, not the fan.

### 3 · `childhood-friends` — "why you and your oldest friend still can't say 'the canoe trip'"
Used in: What gets lost (row 3), the book (1998 spread).
Currently: `47` (two teenagers by a car).

> Late-1990s 35mm consumer film snapshot, two sixteen-year-old boys soaking wet on a
> lakeshore beside an overturned green canoe at dusk, one pointing accusingly at the
> other, both laughing too hard to stand straight, backpacks and a paddle on the
> gravel. Slight flash falloff, Kodak Gold colour, a little soft. 3:2 landscape.

### 4 · `campfire` — "The lake monster" (the collaboration example)
Used in: One memory, everyone's version (the phone), Step one (archive screen), the
book (2024 spread).
Currently: `S01` (family around a campfire).

> Candid phone photograph at a lakeside campsite at blue hour, a grandfather in a
> fleece telling a story by the campfire with both hands raised like claws, an
> eleven-year-old girl wrapped in a blanket staring at the dark lake, her mother
> laughing behind her, a man in the background holding a canoe paddle behind his back.
> Firelight on faces, tents and water behind. Natural colour, 4:3 landscape; keep the
> top third calm (the app crops it under the status bar).

## For families (Pass 11e)

Used on `/for-families` (`Families.tsx`, `OCCASIONS` and the opening). Each slot
currently shows the closest library photo. Send the generated file back with its
slot name and it gets swapped in. House look for all of them: candid, warm, natural
light, real homes, not stock. No text in the image, no visible logos, and no
screens with readable UI (the site draws the app itself).
