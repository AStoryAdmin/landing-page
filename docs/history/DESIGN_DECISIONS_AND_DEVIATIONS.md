# Design decisions

Read `qa/greenfield/DESIGN_STUDIES.md` for the independent composition alternatives considered before production work.

The selected direction is a continuous family documentary. A large typographic opening and single everyday photograph establish the product immediately. Different section structures emerge from the material rather than a repeated left/right template. The substantial brown chapter holds several recollections of one photograph. The chronology uses a restrained date rail, changing image proportions and an open end.

Intentional refinements of the brief:

- Headings use sentence case for editorial clarity; their scale and authored line breaks carry the hierarchy.
- The milestone transition uses native scrolling from restrained formal prints to a much larger everyday photograph. It does not pin the page or impose a scroll-controlled slide system. Phone visitors can inspect the three formal occasions one at a time.
- Three existing fictional lake perspectives are retained, including one story known by the next generation. None is preferred as correct.
- The transparent approved volume and slipcase are used instead of creating new book artwork.
- Motion is restrained: contextual exchange reveal, short discovery transitions, optical button feedback, a slightly shrinking navigation and non-3D reader transitions. Reduced motion removes animation; page content is never gated by it.
- Product interactions live primarily on How it works. The homepage shows only enough interface to make the conversation behavior tangible.

Approved photographs keep natural proportions. No collages, ghost images, fake print aging, decorative rotations or photographic overlays are used. Historical occasion prints alone get a restrained edge.
