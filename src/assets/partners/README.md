# NVIDIA Inception — official badge artwork

A Story Technologies, Inc. was approved into the NVIDIA Inception program on
6 October 2026. The two SVGs beside this file are **NVIDIA's own artwork**,
copied byte for byte out of the badge pack they issued. Nothing here was drawn,
redrawn, optimised or re-exported by us, and nothing here should be.

    nvidia-inception-program-badge-rgb-for-screen.svg       full colour (primary)
    nvidia-inception-program-badge-rgb-1c-blk-for-screen.svg  single colour

Both are 500.4288 x 216 and carry **their own white card** — a white rect under
a black keyline is part of the drawing. That is why no placement on this site
puts a frame, border or panel around the badge: it is already a mounted object,
and a second frame reads as a frame around a frame. What a page owes it is
clear space, which `components/ui/InceptionBadge.tsx` applies as padding.

The print-resolution files (AI/EPS/PDF, CMYK and PMS) are in the original
download and are deliberately not vendored here — the website has no use for
them, and they are large.

## The rules these placements hold to

- The badge is never larger than the A Story logo nearest it. Header logo is
  127x40 and footer 133x42; the badge runs 40px in the hero and 34px in
  the intro and footer. Our Story pairs both marks at 64px, reduced together
  to 44px on phones and 40px on the narrowest screens.
- Never below NVIDIA's 30px digital minimum.
- Colours, proportions and the text inside the badge are never altered.
- In headlines: "NVIDIA Inception Program". Mid-sentence: "the NVIDIA Inception
  program", lowercase p.
- Never "NV Inception", "Inception Program by NVIDIA", "Nvidia", or
  "NVIDIA INCEPTION PROGRAM".
- We claim membership only — never endorsement, investment, partnership or
  backing. Our story says so in as many words.
- The trademark attribution NVIDIA require appears once, at the foot of every
  page, from `NVIDIA_TRADEMARK_NOTE`.
